      Apply a downloaded scheme

      Import the .xccolorscheme file once, then pick it for any session. The scheme is stored like the ones you make yourself, and syncs with your other devices if you use sync.

      1. Open the ⋮ menu at the top right, then Tools > Terminal Color Schemes...

      2. Click ⋮ under the list, choose Import..., and pick the file you downloaded.

      3. The scheme joins the list. Click Close.

      4. Click the session properties button on the toolbar, then Current Session. Default Session sets it for new sessions instead.

      5. Under Appearance, choose the scheme in Color Scheme and click OK.

      6. The terminal takes the new colors right away.

      1. On the home screen, open the menu at the bottom left and tap Terminal Color Schemes.

      2. Tap +, then Import, and pick the downloaded file.

      3. The scheme joins the list.

      4. In Sessions, press and hold a session, then tap Edit.

      5. Under Appearance, pick the scheme in Color Scheme, then tap the check mark to save.

      6. Open the session to see the new colors.
