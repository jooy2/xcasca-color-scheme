Two things differ from the originals, because of how Xcasca draws a terminal:

- **Selection.** Xcasca draws the selection over the text, while these schemes' selection colors are meant to sit behind it. The selection here is each scheme's foreground at 30% opacity, which keeps selected text readable.
- **Search highlights.** The three search highlight colors are the ones the app gives every scheme, since the originals have none.

## Originals

Harbor Night, Ember Glow, Moss Garden, Neon Drift, Graphite, Midnight Plum, Paper Ink, Morning Fog, Sandstone, Dancheong, Velvet Dusk, Lapis, Monsoon, Onyx, Celadon, Cherry Blossom, Lavender Mist, Citrus Grove, Clear Sky, Aubergine and Green Monitor were made for this collection by [jooy2](https://github.com/jooy2) and are released under this repository's [MIT License](LICENSE). In each of them, the foreground stands out from the background by more than 10:1, and every other color by at least 3:1, apart from black on a dark background and white on a light one.
